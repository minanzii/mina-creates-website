About Mina Creates

Hi, I'm Mina! I'm a high school girl who loves making things, from handmade jewelry to little accessories that bring a bit of personality to everyday life. I started Mina Creates to share my love for creating with others, one handmade piece at a time!

My Story

Mina Creates started with something simple: my love for making things. I've always enjoyed experimenting with ideas, playing with colors, and turning little inspirations into something I can hold in my hands. This small business is my way of sharing that creativity with others!

My Design Philosophy

I love playful details, pretty colors, and designs that feel a little special. Every piece is inspired by the things I find cute, fun, or simply too lovely not to create. My goal is to make accessories that bring a little joy to your day!

Handmade With Care

Every piece is handmade by me, with care put into the small details that make each creation unique. Balancing a small business with high school keeps me busy, but creating things I love makes it all worthwhile. Thank you for supporting my little creative adventure!

Shop Local

Mina Creates is a small, locally run business selling handmade creations around Ohio. I love being able to share my work with my local community and meet people who appreciate handmade goods. Every bit of support means so much to me!
