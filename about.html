About Mina Creates

Hi, I'm Mina! I'm a high school girl with a love for making things, from little handmade accessories to pieces that let me share my creativity with others. Mina Creates is my small, handmade business, built from the joy of turning ideas into something real.

My Story

I've always loved creating things with my hands. Whether I'm experimenting with new designs or putting together the little details of a piece, making things has always been something I genuinely enjoy. I started Mina Creates as a way to share that love with other people!

Made With Love

Every piece is handmade by me, with care put into the colors, details, and little touches that make each design special. I love creating things that feel fun, personal, and unique, whether you're treating yourself or finding a gift for someone you love.

Small Business, Big Dreams

Running a small business while being in high school is an adventure of its own! Mina Creates is a passion project that lets me explore my creativity, learn new things, and share what I make beyond my own desk.

Find Me Around Ohio!

Mina Creates is a locally run business based in Ohio. I sell my handmade creations at local markets and events, where I get to meet people who enjoy handmade goods as much as I do. Thank you for supporting my little business and being part of its journey!
